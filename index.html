Technical Methodology & Heuristics

ProofLens combines deterministic rule heuristics with a plug-and-play AI semantic inspection adapter.

1. Advance Fee Demands (Rule ID: FIN-01)

Scrutinizes text for requirements involving security deposits, laptop courier payments, training material charges, or documentation processing fees. Formal enterprise employment practices prohibit candidates from paying onboarding expenses.

2. Free Mail Impersonation (Rule ID: IDN-02)

Detects the utilization of public mail services (e.g., Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook) by entities purporting to represent established enterprise organizations. Legitimate corporate recruitment is conducted via registered organizational domains with SPF/DKIM validation.

3. Suspicious Domain Classification (Rule ID: NET-03)

Identifies non-standard, low-cost top-level domains (.xyz, .top, .work, .buzz) and temporary hosting services frequently leveraged for short-lived credential harvesting and phishing pages.

4. Process Anomaly & Direct Selection (Rule ID: PROC-04)

Evaluates offers extended without formal skills assessment, coding examinations, or interviews. Authentic technical roles enforce structured evaluation procedures.

5. Modular AI Semantic & Visual Inspection (Rule ID: LLM-06)

Utilizes a plug-and-play model adapter compatible with Gemini 2.5 Flash, Groq Llama-3.3, and OpenAI to detect contextual social engineering, psychological urgency, and fraudulent offer screenshots that evade static regex patterns.

Privacy Policy & Data Handling Specification

Effective Date: October 2026 | Production Compliance Standard

1. Zero Persistent Data Retention

ProofLens processes submitted text and uploaded documents entirely in-memory within your active browser session. No pasted communications, email headers, extracted names, API keys, or personal identifying data (PII) are stored on disk or written to remote databases.

2. Client-Side & Ephemeral Execution

Deterministic heuristic algorithms execute locally in your client environment. When the optional AI Deep Inspection layer is enabled with a user-supplied key, the payload is transmitted directly over TLS to the selected model endpoint for ephemeral inference.

3. No Training on User Data

Your submitted notices and confidential offer documents are never harvested, aggregated, or utilized by ProofLens to train external machine learning models or commercial classifiers.
